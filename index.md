I study how polarization and political violence shape democratic representation and participation. My work in comparative politics and political behavior also examines voter mobilization and democratic resilience. I use field and survey experiments, quasi-experimental designs, and quantitative text analysis using large language models.

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the [WZB Transformations of Democracy Unit](https://www.wzb.eu/en/research/dynamics-of-political-systems/transformations-of-democracy), directed by Daniel Ziblatt.

[All publications and working papers](/research/)

## About

As a postdoc, I visited Nuffield College at the University of Oxford (Summer 2024) and the [Center for European Studies](https://ces.fas.harvard.edu/people/fabio-ellger) at Harvard University (Fall 2022). In 2022, I obtained my doctoral degree at the [Chair of Comparative Political Behavior](https://www.sowi.hu-berlin.de/en/lehrbereiche-en/comparative-political-behavior/team/team-comparative-political-behavior) of Humboldt University Berlin and graduated from [DYNAMICS](https://www.sowi.hu-berlin.de/en/dynamics/people/Alumni), a joint PhD program of Humboldt University and the Hertie School.

My work has appeared in *The Journal of Politics*, *British Journal of Political Science*, *Comparative Political Studies*, and *West European Politics*. In 2025, my research was awarded the *Gordon Smith and Vincent Wright Memorial Prize* for outstanding work appearing in *West European Politics*.

I also [teach](/teaching/) classes on political behavior, research design, and applied research methods.
